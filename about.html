ℹ️ About Me

I am Sujal Timalsina from Birgunj, currently living in Lalitpur pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering at Pulchowk Campus. I have been passionate about mathematics and science from a very young age. After completing my SEE from Jeetpur Public English School, I studied +2 Science (Computer) inside Kathmandu valley, and then prepared a bit for IOE entrance.

I got admitted to Pulchowk Campus in 2079 B.S. The classes were delayed by 6 months due to COVID-19. The journey has been a little bit here and there. And, tbh I am a very lazy person. Looking back, the last three years have passed incredibly fast. It feels like I was studying +2 just a few days ago.

And yes, that's it. That's almost everything about me.

Background

Over the past few years, I’ve spent a lot of time exploring ideas beyond the classroom. The traditional exam-focused system was too boring and the one-way lecture style didn’t align with how I prefer to learn. I did follow the curriculum but mostly through discussions with friends and reading books. I spent most of my time doing the things that I liked to do like playing chess, football, and occasionally doing some programming and research.

However, most of my projects were only 70-90% complete, with the final versions just ending up in my head. Creating this website is my way of changing that: a place to complete, document, and share the projects that I want to bring to life. Also, I get a place to drop all the crazy ideas that are in my head waiting to be implemented.
