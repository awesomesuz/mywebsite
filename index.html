✨ Welcome

I am a lover of machines, who also builds software for them occasionally. This website is a personal space where I document my interests, projects, and experiments related to engineering systems. My curiosity spans physical machines, computer systems, and the software that connects them.

What I do

I'm currently a third-year Computer Engineering student at Pulchowk Campus. My days are filled with code, circuits, and curiosity. I enjoy building software (web dev, game prototyping, random experiments) and understanding how machines tick – from the silicon up.

But my interests don't stop there. I am deeply into mathematics, physics, drawing, and the kind of precise thinking they demand. Whether it's sketching a mechanical part or solving a differential equation, I love the process of turning abstract ideas into something tangible.

Tools I Work With

  • Editors & IDEs: VS Code, Android Studio, Arduino IDE

  • Graphics / Game Dev: Raylib, OpenGL, WinBGI, basic computer graphics
